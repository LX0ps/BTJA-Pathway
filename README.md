# BTJA-Pathway

> **Notes, lab write-ups, and practical exercises from the Centri Blue Team Junior Analyst pathway.**

---

## 🛡️ About Centri
**Centri** is a platform dedicated to cybersecurity education. They offer a range of free courses with certificates in core security domains such as digital forensics, network analysis, threat hunting, and more.

---

## 🎯 About This Repository
Maintained by **lx0ps**, this repository documents my progress, write-ups, and lab notes for the **Blue Team Junior Analyst (BTJA) Pathway Certificate** offered by Centri.

The pathway consists of 6 core hands-on modules:

- **Dark Web Operations**
- **Network Analysis**
- **OSINT**
- **Digital Forensics**
- **Threat Hunting**
- **Vulnerability Management**

Completing these six courses awards the official **Blue Team Junior Analyst Pathway Certificate**.
